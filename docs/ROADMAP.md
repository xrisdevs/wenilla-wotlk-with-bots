# Arena and PvP bot direction

The aim is proper playable arenas with bots that make understandable, increasingly strong PvP decisions.

1. **Reliable matches:** legal spawns behind gates, correct gear/weapon visuals and stats, controlled countdowns, spectator isolation, disconnect/recovery handling and repeatable setup.
2. **Measured baselines:** headless fixed-composition runs across maps; record wins, duration, damage/healing, deaths, idle time and stuck movement. Preserve seeds/configuration and aggregate results without publishing player identities.
3. **Better decisions:** target selection, positioning/line of sight, healer protection, peels, interrupt timing, defensive cooldowns, coordinated crowd control and diminishing-return awareness.
4. **Playable arena system:** better team balance, reconnect behavior, persistent match results, rating-based matchmaking and replay/inspection tools. Current player queues are FIFO, not rated matchmaking.
5. **Client quality:** stock-compatible tooltips/talents, correct camera/input behavior, browser performance, accessible UI scaling and desktop parity.

Use controlled comparisons before changing tuning defaults. Measure failures as well as wins; avoid optimizing only for one composition or mirror match. The current presets and bots are a starting point, not a claim of competitive arena intelligence.
